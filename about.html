Introduction

I'm an Associate DevOps Engineer & Full-Stack Developer with 4+ years of experience building enterprise applications, automating workflows, and optimizing data pipelines. Currently at AAA Auto Club Enterprises through Workforce Opportunity Services — building full-stack features in C#/.NET & React, automating data workflows with Python, and co-developing SSIS packages for SQL Server reporting.

As a first-generation college graduate and one of Northeastern University's first Miami campus graduates, my path from financial services to software engineering is driven by curiosity and determination. I hold a Chase Scholarship from BrainStation (Top 25%), where I built production-ready React/Node.js applications across 15+ Agile user stories with a perfect 4.0 GPA.

I bring a rare combination of technical depth and business acumen — having led data analysis and process automation at scale (99.8% accuracy across 5,200+ loan files annually), delivered technical solutions at Verizon and Amazon, and spoken at React Miami, eMerge Americas, and Women's History Month events.
